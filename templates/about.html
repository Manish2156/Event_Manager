ABOUT US

Making College Events Easier to Discover

College Event Manager is a platform designed to bring college events together in one place. From hackathons and workshops to cultural activities and sports events, students can easily discover what's happening around their campus.

Why We Built This

College event information is often scattered across WhatsApp groups, social media, notice boards and different college communities. Our platform brings this information together in one convenient place.

What You Can Do

Discover Events

Find upcoming events happening in your college and explore them by category.

Explore Categories

Browse events based on your interests such as Hackathons, Sports, Cultural Activities, Workshops and more.

Stay Updated

Keep track of important event dates, venues and other information.

Personalized Experience

Your favourite categories can help the platform show events that match your interests.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a connected college community where students never miss an opportunity simply because they didn't hear about it.
